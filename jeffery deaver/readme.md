## Jeffery Deaver books

### Lincoln Rhyme Series

**Follows quadriplegic forensic criminalist Lincoln Rhyme and NYPD officer Amelia Sachs.**

- The Bone Collector (1997)
- The Coffin Dancer (1998)
- The Empty Chair (2000)
- The Stone Monkey (2002)
- The Vanished Man (2003)
- The Twelfth Card (2005)
- The Cold Moon (2006) (Introduces Kathryn Dance)
- The Broken Window (2008)
- The Burning Wire (2010)
- The Kill Room (2013)
- The Skin Collector (2014)
- The Steel Kiss (2016)
- The Burial Hour (2017)
- The Cutting Edge (2018)
- The Midnight Lock (2021)
- The Watchmaker's Hand (2023)
- The Collateral Heart (2026)

### Kathryn Dance Series

**Focuses on a California body language and kinesics expert.**

- The Sleeping Doll (2007)
- Roadside Crosses (2009)
- XO (2012)
- Solitude Creek (2015)

### Colter Shaw Series

**Features a professional reward seeker and tracker.**

- The Never Game (2019)
- The Goodbye Man (2020)
- The Final Twist (2021)
- Hunting Time (2022)
- South of Nowhere (2025)

### Standalone Thrillers

- Mistress of Justice (1992)
- The Lesson of Her Death (1993)
- Praying for Sleep (1994)
- A Maiden's Grave (1995)
- The Devil's Teardrop (1999) (Lincoln Rhyme cameo)
- Speaking in Tongues (2000)
- The Blue Nowhere (2001)
- Garden of Beasts (2004)
- The Bodies Left Behind (2008)
- Edge (2010)
- Carte Blanche (2011) (Official James Bond continuation novel)
- The October List (2013) (Written in reverse chronological order)
