## Keigo Higashino books

### Detective Kaga series
**Best reading order: I recommend reading them chronologically because Kaga's personal story develops across the books, although you won't be lost if you start with Malice or Newcomer.**
- Graduation
- The Forest of Sleep
- Malice
- The Red Finger
- Newcomer
- A Death in Tokyo
- The Final Curtain
- The Thread of Hope
- You Killed Someone

### Detective Galileo series
**These are also individually solvable mysteries, so you don't strictly need to read them in order.**
- Detective Galileo — short stories
- Precognition Dream — short stories
- [The Devotion of Suspect X](https://github.com/pirate-barbosa/ebooks/blob/main/keigo%20higashino/The%20Devotion%20of%20Suspect%20X.epub)
