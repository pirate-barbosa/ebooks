1. 🧠 Amos Decker / Memory Man — 7 books

This is one of my top recommendations from Baldacci.

Memory Man (2015)
The Last Mile (2016)
The Fix (2017)
The Fallen (2018)
Redemption (2019)
Walk the Wire (2020)
Long Shadows (2022)

Amos Decker has a phenomenal memory and can remember virtually everything. The series is more mystery/investigation-heavy than some of Baldacci's other books.

2. 🎯 Will Robie — 5 novels + short story
The Innocent (2012)
The Hit (2013)
The Target (2014)
The Guilty (2015)
End Game (2017)

Short story: Bullseye (2014)

This is probably the series I'd recommend if you want assassins, action, espionage and fast pacing.

3. 🪖 John Puller — 4 books
Zero Day (2011)
The Forgotten (2012)
The Escape (2014)
No Man's Land (2016)

Puller is a combat veteran and Army investigator. Very good choice if you like military thrillers.

4. 🕵️ Atlee Pine — 4 books
Long Road to Mercy (2018)
A Minute to Midnight (2019)
Daylight (2020)
Mercy (2021)

This series has a strong overarching mystery involving Atlee's missing twin sister.

5. 🏛️ Camel Club — 5 books
The Camel Club (2005)
The Collectors (2006)
Stone Cold (2007)
Divine Justice (2008)
Hell's Corner (2010)

Oliver Stone and the Camel Club deal with conspiracies, intelligence agencies, politics and government secrets.

6. 👥 King & Maxwell — 6 books
Split Second (2003)
Hour Game (2004)
Simple Genius (2007)
First Family (2009)
The Sixth Man (2011)
King and Maxwell (2013)

Sean King and Michelle Maxwell are former Secret Service agents turned private investigators.

7. 🎩 Aloysius Archer — 3 books
One Good Deed (2019)
A Gambling Man (2021)
Dream Town (2022)

This one is different from Baldacci's modern thrillers: 1940s–50s America, private investigation, crime and a classic detective feel.

8. 🚆 Travis Devine / The 6:20 Man — 4 books
The 6:20 Man (2022)
The Edge (2023)
To Die For (2024)
All In (2026)

Travis Devine is a former Army Ranger working in the financial world. This is one of Baldacci's newer series.

9. 🕶️ Shaw — 2 books
The Whole Truth (2008)
Deliver Us from Evil (2010)

International espionage/thriller style.

10. 🧙 Vega Jane — 4 books
The Finisher (2014)
The Keeper (2015)
The Width of the World (2017)
The Stars Below (2019)

This is Baldacci's fantasy/young-adult series rather than his usual adult thrillers.

11. 🍟 Freddy and the French Fries — 2 books
Fries Alive! (2005)
The Mystery of Silas Finklebean (2006)

Children's series.

12. 🕵️ Walter Nash — 2 books
Nash Falls (2025)
Hope Rises (2026)

His newest series.
